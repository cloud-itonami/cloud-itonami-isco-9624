# Business Model: Water and Firewood Collection Site Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9624`
- ISCO-08: `9624`
- Occupation: Water and Firewood Collectors
- Social impact: worker-safety, water-quality, resource-sustainability

## Customer

- rural/peri-urban water-and-firewood-collection cooperatives
- community water-point and woodlot management organizations
- independent water-and-firewood-collection crews

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- collection-log/progress-record logging
- collection-equipment procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to site safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a collection-work-execution decision (e.g.
  finalizing the collection operation), ever
- no direct finalization of a site-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a water source safe for collection), ever
- no override of a site safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (physical-exertion/carrying-load hazard,
  terrain hazard, water-source-quality concern) always route to human
  sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
